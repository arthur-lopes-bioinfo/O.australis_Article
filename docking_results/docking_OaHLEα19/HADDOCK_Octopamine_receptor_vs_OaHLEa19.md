# Molecular Docking Results — Octopamine Receptor vs OaHLEα19

## 1. General Analysis Information

Molecular docking between the **octopamine receptor** and the enterotoxin **OaHLEα19** was performed using the **HADDOCK 2.4** server.

The run was successfully completed, including the *post-processing* stage.

The probable extracellular interaction region of the octopamine receptor was defined using the following residue ranges:

- **Pro78–Cys107**
- **Phe156–Thr170**
- **Pro194–Gly204**

These residues were used to guide docking toward the proposed extracellular interaction region of the receptor.

### Clustering Summary

- **Clustered structures:** 27
- **Number of clusters:** 5
- **Percentage of water-refined models included in clusters:** 13%
- **Maximum number of models considered for clustering by HADDOCK:** 200

HADDOCK ranks clusters primarily according to the **HADDOCK score**, with more negative values generally associated with more favorable solutions. The **Z-score** indicates how many standard deviations a cluster score differs from the mean score of all clusters, with more negative values indicating a relatively more favorable cluster.

## 4. Selection of the Best Conformation

Based on the parameters provided by HADDOCK, **Cluster 1** was selected as the most representative cluster for the interaction between the octopamine receptor and the OaHLEα19 enterotoxin.

This cluster exhibited:

- the **lowest HADDOCK score** (-81.5 ± 6.8);
- the **best Z-score** (-1.0);
- the **most favorable electrostatic energy** (-305.1 ± 34.0);
- the **largest cluster size** (10 structures).

Although Cluster 5 showed more favorable van der Waals and desolvation energies and a larger buried surface area, Cluster 1 remained the top-ranked solution according to the overall HADDOCK score and Z-score.

Therefore, the highest-ranked structure belonging to **Cluster 1** was considered the primary candidate conformation for subsequent structural analyses of the receptor–toxin interface.
